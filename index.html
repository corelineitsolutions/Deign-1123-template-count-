How extras count. Only images count. On a working day, images above the target add to extras and images below it are taken away. With a target of 18: Monday 19 (+1), Tuesday 20 (+2), Wednesday 16 (−2) gives 1 net extra.

Holidays, weekly offs and leave have no target and nothing is deducted. Images made on those days count fully as extra.

Not logged working days are flagged but not deducted. Videos are tracked against their own target and never change extras.

Winner. The highest net extra above 0 wins that week's prize; equal totals share it. The weekly report opens after the last working day of the week (normally on Sunday).
